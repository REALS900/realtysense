End-to-End Fiduciary PEB & Warehouse Consulting

Single-window Owner’s Representative governance across Odisha—protecting your capital from bare land and GO-SWIFT clearances to steel auditing and Grade-A corporate leasing.

WHY INDEPENDENT PEB OVERSIGHT MATTERS

Building a Grade-A warehouse along Odisha’s highway corridors is no longer about erecting a basic metal shed. National 3PL, e-commerce, and manufacturing tenants reject facilities that fail strict structural, flooring, and fire safety audits. Without independent technical governance, landowners lose crores to inflated steel tonnage quotes, civil-to-steel anchor bolt misalignments, and regulatory delays.

OUR 5-STAGE END-TO-END HANDHOLDING

Stage 1: Land, Title & Access Feasibility

Verifying non-tribal clear title, approach-road heavy container turning radii, soil bearing capacity (SBC), and zoning viability across Khordha, Cuttack, Jajpur/Kalinganagar, and Jharsuguda corridors before capital is committed.

Stage 2: Statutory & GO-SWIFT Regulatory Governance

Managing land conversion, industrial layout approvals, Fire NOC compliance, and digital Level 2 clearances through Odisha’s GO-SWIFT single-window portal without bureaucratic bottlenecks.

Stage 3: PEB Fabricator Vetting & Steel Tonnage Auditing

Floating transparent RFPs to reputed PEB fabricators, auditing structural design drawings to eliminate raw steel tonnage over-inflation, and locking in strict commercial delivery milestones.

Stage 4: Civil-to-Steel Interface & Grade-A Spec Supervision

Supervising the critical anchor-bolt interface between civil foundations and fabricated columns—ensuring institutional 12m clear heights, FM2 laser-screed flooring, PUF insulation, and solar-ready rafters.

Stage 5: Corporate Leasing & LRD Yield Realization

Handing over an audit-ready facility and executing long-term Triple-Net (NNN) leases with Grade-A tenants—or structuring Lease Rental Discounting (LRD) to unlock your construction equity.
